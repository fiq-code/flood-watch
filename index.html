Warning (Amaran)
MET Malaysia weather warnings (continuous rain, thunderstorms) via data.gov.my. Flooding possible in 1–3 days.
Pre-flood (Pra-banjir)
A JPS river station within the set radius of a facility is at alert or warning level. Flooding may start within hours.
Flood (Banjir)
A JPS river station within the radius is at danger level, or a logged road is closed.
Recovery (Pemulihan)
A station that reached warning or danger level in the last 72 hours is dropping, or a logged road has reopened.
Data sources
Weather and river levels: data.gov.my Open API (MET Malaysia, JPS). Facilities: OpenStreetMap. Manual checks: Public InfoBanjir, JKR eBencana, LLM highway CCTV, NADMA Portal Bencana.
